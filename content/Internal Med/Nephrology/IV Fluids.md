albumin
	low albumin associated with worse outcomes in ICU 
	but does albumin replacement help?
		- SAFE trial = no diff in outcomes overall, higher mortality in traumatic brain injury, in septic patients had mortality benefit
		- ALBIOS trial = aimed to get albumin lvl to 30 (excluded cirrhotic pts), showed no mortality benefit, but 2/3rd pts had septic shock and in that population, mortality was 6.3% lower compared to crystalloid BUT (see below)
		- 2026 JAMA Open trial = septic shock pts only, albumin vs crystalloid, no difference in 90d mortality
