1. ↓ Production

	- Nutrition (marrow supp) - EtOH, B12, folate; macrocytosis, glossitis
	- Drugs (marrow supp) - other cytopenias; chemo, linez (>2wk), valproate, septra (prolonged) 
	- Neoplasm (marrow infiltration) - Bsx, other cytopenias, LAN, HSM, blasts on smear (leukemia, MDS, marrow mets)
	- Other - Cirrhosis, aplastic anemia, some VWD, congenital
2. ↑ Destruction
	- Immune mediated 
		- ITP - isolated, <100, dx exclusion, bimodal (young or 60s); 2° ITP - known SLE, APLS, CLL, or CVID
		- Drug induced immune - abx (vanco, sulfa, pen)/ thiazide/ quin/ rifamp/ carbamaz/ GPIIb/IIIa inh 
		- HIT - on heparin 5–10 days (or sooner with prior exposure) with platelet fall >50%, new arterial or venous thrombosis; 4T score and HIT antibody/serotonin release assay
		- Evans syndrome
	- Consumptive → typically w hemolytic anemia; in this bucket is the MAHAs (↑hemolytic markers, schistocytes - though may not be present in low grade hemolysis, but need hemolytic markers) 
		- TMAs
			- TTP - neurologic changes, renal impairment, fever
			- non-TTP
				- HUS - E coli GI infxn diarrhea, shiga toxin, more severe AKI 
				- aHUS - complement dysregulation, Ø diarrhea 
				- secondary TMA - infxn, malig, AI
		- other (shearing in vessels but Ø from microthrombi)
			- DIC - critical illness, ↑INR ↑PTT ↓fibrinogen, ↑Dimer, shistocytes
			- Mechanical (technically macroangiopathic) - prosthetic valve, severe aortic stenosis, ECMO/ dialysis, IABP; schistocytes from shear stress
			- Malignant HTN
			- HELPP
3. Sequestration
	- Splenic sequestration (hypersplenism) - patient with portal hypertension from cirrhosis, splenomegaly on exam/imaging; mild-moderate thrombocytopenia with other cytopenias, platelet count corrects with splenectomy
	- Massive transfusion / dilutional — patient receiving large-volume resuscitation or multiple units of packed red cells without proportional platelet replacement
4. Pseudo-thrombocytopenia
	- EDTA tube plt clumping - isolated plt low, asx, clump on smear, rpt in citrate tube
