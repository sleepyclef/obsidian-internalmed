Complementary imaging 
- usually starts with TTE and CT chest maybe then can get additional imaging
- PET will show uptake if the lesion is malignant and metabolically active - but IE can also have uptake 

Lipomatous septal hyertrophy - can see on TTE, usually get cMRI and look at LGE (LEG = whatever gado stays will appear as white whereas normal myocardium will be grey; LGE usually indicates fibrosis/scar - to diff if scar is ischemic; ischemic will start subendocardial and follow a typical vessel territory) 
- how to diagnose LSH on cMRI is with T2 FS 

if tumor is >1cm and not moving too much, should be looking at it via cMRI
