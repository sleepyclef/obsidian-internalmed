cut off for EF is different for MR since you expect the EF to actually be 65-70% since there is a low pressure release chamber (the LA) that the LV can empty into 

why might acute severe MR look less severe on echo by color doppler?
Acute severe MR: small, noncompliant LA → LA pressure shoots up → early LV–LA pressure equalization → “severe MR, surprisingly small-looking jet.”
this is also why you may not hear a murmur 
the picture will be dominated by florid heart failure, pulm edema/ shock, likely needing hemodynamic support
