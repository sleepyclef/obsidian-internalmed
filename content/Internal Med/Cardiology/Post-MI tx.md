<u>Statin</u> - atorva 80mg, rosuva 40mg 
	==start early for all, even pre-PCI== 
	CCS = tx goal <1.8; ESC 2019 = tx goal <1.4 and 50% red. 
	if alr on statin, check recent LDL, if not on target then intensify regimen (either ↑ dose if not at max, or add other agent)

<u>ACE/ARB</u> - ramipril 2.5mg, lisinopril 2.5mg, valsartan 20
	==w/in 24h in all== patients 
	c/i to starting = hypotension (SBP<90), renal function, hyperK, hx angioedema 
	when to give for sure 
		+++benefit esp if anterior STEMI 
		EF<40 even w/out sx (LV dysfxn), or nl EF but HF sx (HFpEF post-MI only)
		HTN, CKD, DM
	otherwise, reasonable even if no other indication
	use ARB (valsartan, candesartan) if intolerant of ACE 
	lexidrug dosing
		-ramipril → initial: 2.5 mg once daily; titrate slowly based on tolerability and response up to 20 mg/day in 1 or 2 divided doses.
		-lisonpril → initial: 2.5 to 10 mg once daily (depending on initial blood pressure); titrate slowly based on tolerability and response up to 40 mg/day.
		-enlapril → initial: 2.5 to 5 mg in 1 or 2 divided doses (depending on initial blood pressure); titrate slowly based on tolerability and response up to 40 mg/day in 1 or 2 divided doses.
		-captopril → Initial: 6.25 mg; if tolerated, increase to 12.5 mg 3 times daily; then increase to 25 mg 3 times daily during next several days and then gradually increase over next several weeks to target dose of 50 mg 3 times daily as tolerated.

<u>β-blocker</u> - metoprolol 25mg (bid/q6h if tartare, OD succ), carvedilol 6.25mg BID, bisoprolol 5mg, atenolol  
	thus far, guidelines recommend starting ==w/in 24h for all== 
		- key - BB should be given to post ACS patients with HF or LVEF<40 and considered in all patients
	c/i to starting = HR<60/ AVB (1° if PR>240ms), SBP<100, ADHF, cardio shock, active asthma
	when to give for sure 
		- EF<40% (HFrEF) - part of GDMT; if EF unkown and pending TTE, give it (unless c/i) and r/a after or at discharge 
		- other indication for BB 
	when BB may not be needed?
		- if no other indications ie. Ø HTN, Ø ↓EF, Ø CKD, Ø angina/CAD, Ø arrhythmia, Ø WMA on TTE
		- REDUCE-AMI (NEJM 2024): EF>50% & no other ind., no added benefit of β-blocker 
	continue BB 6+ mo post MI if normal EF?
		- ABYSS (NEJM 2024): stopping BB 6mo post-MI w EF>40% was statistically non-inferior for a broad composite (death/MI/stroke/CV hospitalization), but showed more death/MI/stroke. This happened because fewer hospitalizations in the stop group diluted the overall composite, making it look non-inferior, even though the hard outcomes worsened. ∴ while technically “non-inferior,” most clinicians remain cautious about stopping.

<u>MRA</u> - spironolactone, eplerenone
	==if LVEF<40== and HF sx (ie. HFrEF) or DM (given no renal failure Cr>221 or hyperkalemia K>5)
		- part of GDMT for HFrEF when pt is symptomatic (ie. stage C/D HFrEF)
		- post-MI start MRA if EF<40 and DM even if Ø sx HF ie. “LV dysfxn, stage B HF, NYHA n/a” (or obv if HF sx as above point)
