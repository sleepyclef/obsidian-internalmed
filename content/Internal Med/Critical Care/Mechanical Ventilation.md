<u>Physiology</u> 
Normal person 
	RR = 12 
	spend 1/3rd time in insp, 2/3rd in expiration 
compliance 
	high compliance (need small pressure for big change in volume) = eg. COPD / emphysema 
	low compliance (stiff ling; takes lots of pressure ) = eg. ILD/ IPF, ARDS
	hysteresis curve
		relationship between pressure and volume based on compliance 
		need lots of pressure to overcome the initial compliance (balloon is harder to blow in the beginning)
			this is why we always have a bit or residual volume 
		you can only control one thing, either the pressure or the volume

ventilator modes 
pressure
	pressure control 
	pressure support/ spontaneous  
volume
	volume control 
	(volume support/ spontaneous) - pretty much doesn't exist based on how volume works

PEEP
	equivalent to/ serves the purpose of residual volume in normal physiology → to keep the alveoli open/ prevent collapse
	the problem is *how much* peep is required

ventilation = strictly speaking it is how good at clearing CO2
