indicates alveoli are partially filled with fluid (pus, blood, edema)
appears gray on CT
preservation of bronchial and vascular markings
contrast to consolidation which is if alveoli are fully filled with fluid and appears white 
false positive if expiratory CT

etiology based on pattern most useful 
diffuse = bilateral, widespread most of the lobes, no zone spared
	infectious
		viral PNA (flu, covid, rsv) = resp swabs
		PJP PNA = LDH, BD-glucan, CRP, induced sputum PCR, BAL PCR
		CMV PNA = CMV PCR blood +/- BAL PCR
		atypical bacterial PNA (legionella, mycoplasma) = Na, bcx
	edema
		pulm edema = vol status, BNP
		ARDS =sepsis/aspiration/pancreatitis/trauma, P/F <300
	hemorrhage (DAH)
		ANCA vasculitis = hemoptysis (absent in 1/3), hematuria, sinus dz, ANCAs
		anti-GBM = anti-GBM Ab, hematuria/AKI,
	drug-induced/ toxin
		drug pneumonitis = amio, nitro, MTX, bleo, EGFR TKI, checkpoint inhibitors 
		radiation pneumonitis = mostly patchy but can be diffuse
	ILD
		HSP (hypersens) = bird, mold, hottub; can be diffuse/patchy/mosaic attenuation; lymphocytic BAL

patchy = several separate areas, some normal lung
	infectious
		viral PNA (flu, covid, rsv) = resp swabs
		PJP PNA (early) = LDH, BD-glucan, CRP, induced sputum PCR, BAL PCR
		CMV PNA = CMV PCR blood +/- BAL PCR
		atypical bacterial PNA (legionella, mycoplasma) = Na, bcx
		aspiration = dysphagia/decreased LOC, dependent segments
	edema
		pulm edema (early/asymmetric) = vol status, BNP, echo/POCUS
	hemorrhage
		early/mild DAH = Hb trend, hemoptysis, ANCA, anti-GBM, serial bloody BAL aliquots
	drug-induced/ toxin  
		drug pneumonitis = temporal link to drug (amio, nitro, MTX, bleo, EGFR TKI, checkpoint inhibitors)  
		radiation pneumonitis = geographic, conforms to RT field, 4-12 wks after RT
	ILD
		OP = fails antibiotics, migratory, reversed halo  
		HSP = exposure hx, mosaic, air trapping on expiratory CT  
		eosinophilic PNA = CBC eos, BAL eos >25%

focal = single area in one lobe, if round (= ground glass nodule), otherwise focal GGO, can have halo sign
(etiologies)
	infectious
		early lobar/segmental PNA = fever, resp sx, cultures; resolves on repeat CT
		aspiration = dysphagia/decreased LOC, dependent segment
		invasive aspergillosis (halo) = neutropenic, galactomannan, BD-glucan; also mucor, candida
		reversed halo (atoll) = OP, mucor, PJP, TB
		focal infection/inflammation (GG nodule) = resolves on repeat CT at 3 mo
	hemorrhage 
		focal hemorrhage = Hb, hemoptysis, trauma/biopsy/anticoagulation hx
		GPA (halo) = PR3-ANCA, sinus dz, hematuria
	ILD
		OP (focal, reversed halo) = fails antibiotics
		focal fibrosis/scar (GG nodule) = stable on serial CT
	neoplastic
		adenocarcinoma spectrum (AAH, AIS, MIA, lepidic) = persistent GG nodule >3 mo, serial CT per Fleischner, resect if growing or new solid component
		hemorrhagic mets (halo) = RCC, melanoma, choriocarcinoma, angiosarcoma
	vascular
		pulmonary infarct = wedge-shaped, peripheral, pleuritic pain, CTPA

peripheral/ subpleural = outer third of lung abutting the pleura
	infectious
		viral/COVID PNA = bilateral, lower zone, subpleural; resp swabs
		drug-induced/ toxin
		drug pneumonitis (NSIP/OP pattern) = temporal link to drug
		EVALI = vaping hx (THC), GI sx, fever, subpleural sparing
	ILD
		NSIP / CTD-ILD = basal, subpleural sparing; ANA, ENA, myositis panel, CK
		OP = peripheral/peribronchial, reversed halo, fails antibiotics
		chronic eosinophilic PNA = CBC eos, BAL eos >25%, "photo negative of edema", upper lobes
		early UIP/IPF = basal, reticulation > GGO, traction bronchiectasis; HRCT, MDD
	vascular
		pulmonary infarct = wedge-shaped, CTPA

bronchovascular = cuff around bronchi/ vessels, 
	infectious
		aspiration/bronchopneumonia = bronchocentric, dependent; cultures
	edema
		pulm edema = peribronchovascular cuffing + septal thickening, effusions, BNP
	ILD
		OP = peribronchovascular consolidation/GGO, fails antibiotics
		sarcoidosis = perilymphatic nodules (bronchovascular, subpleural, septal), hilar nodes, ACE/Ca
		LIP = Sjogren, HIV, CVID, thin-walled cysts
		eosinophilic PNA / EGPA = CBC eos, asthma, ANCA (EGPA ~40%)
	neoplastic
		lymphoma / leukemic infiltrate = heme malignancy, LDH, nodes
		lymphangitic carcinomatosis = known adenoCA, septal thickening, often asymmetric
		invasive mucinous adenoCA = bronchorrhea, multifocal
		Kaposi sarcoma = HIV, flame-shaped, skin lesions
