Also need:
- Sterile gown
- Non sterile towel to rest pt wrist onto on the side of the bed to elevate it
- Lido (w/out epi) with v small insulin needle (28G) or 5-10cc syringe with 23/small gauge and blunt red tip
If US guided:
- Sterile probe cover
- Sterile gel

<u>Steps</u>

Set up ur table (sterile field)
1. Open up pack of sterile towels onto a table, this will be the sterile field
2. Can open up gown pack as well and use as sterile field too
3. Start unpacking sterile equipment
	1. angiocaths
	2. guidewire
	3. freezing needle + syringe, predraw the lido
	4. gauze
	5. tegaderm

Set up pt arm and clean
1. Have it on side of bed next to their torso, elevate bed
2. put blue pad under arm
3. tape their hand to the side of the bed
4. clean large area with chlorhex

Get sterile, gown+gloves
1. Open up gloves onto gown pack sterile field
2. Sanitize hand
3. Put on gown by finding arm holes, go through but not fully out then ask someone to tie
4. Put on gloves by using the arm sleeves in the gown 

Prep sterile area
1. If have only 2 towels using diamond technique by grabbing 2 diagonal ends and making small triangle
2. If have more towels then just lay them out to have a big sterile area
3. Take your guidewire and lay it out on patient body in sterile area so it is ready to go, have soft tip pointing towards patient
4. Put angiocath on sterile towel area as well so its ready

Freezing
1. Give small amount of freezing 1-2cc where you would prob inject; not too much so vessel doesn’t vasospasm

Angiocath insertion
1. Palpate the pulse with 3 fingers with non-dominant hand, don’t go too distal, leave space to put in needle
2. Ensure that at the index finger most distal you have the strongest pulse, take time and feel where it is until confident its position and trajectory
3. KEEP HAND ON PULSE FROM NOW ON UNTIL ANGIOCATH NEEDLE IS OUT
4. Grab angiocath with dominant hand and go close to the index finger and insert superficially but a bit steep, try to feel the trajectory
5. Once get first flash, go in more till not have it anymore
6. Hold angiocath securely with thumb and middle finger so index finger is ready to plug the hole when back in vessel
7. Pull needle out
8. Then slowly pull back on catheter till get pulsatile flash again,
9. HOLD CATHETER IN PLACE DO NOT LET GO - hold it with thumb and middle finger and plug with index finger

Wiring and connecting
1. Insert guidewire, make sure no resistance
2. Once good length of wire is in, then slide the angiocath further in
3. Take wire out and plug again with index
4. Connect the apparatus onto angiocath

Cleaning
1. Wipe around area with gauze whilst securing angiocath with left hand
2. Put on tegaderm
