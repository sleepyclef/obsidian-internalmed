<u>With Para Kit - can do without assist </u>
Get para kit, towel, 1 abdo pad, lido (although have some inside para kit as well), sterile gloves, blunt fill needle, +/- smaller catheter if suspect superficial 

Prepping
- US and mark spot
- Wipe US gel with the abdo pad/ towel 
- Lay down abdo pad on side 
- Clear table and open para kit onto table and take it out of packaging (no need to be sterile)
- While unfolding, take the green holders to move stuff around before putting on sterile gloves (the tip of this pincher is sterile) 
- Move stuff with green graspers to expose the pocket for the lidocaine 
- Put the green graspers on the edge of the sterile area making sure the tip is in the sterile field but rest of it laying down on table
- Open lido and squeeze it out in pocket 
- Put sterile gloves down on the bed or somewhere else 
- Open and put on sterile gloves

Cleaning & draping
- Crack the first chlorhex stick and shake it while in package (to avoid splashing) 
- Open it and start cleaning
- Repeat with second stick 
- Open drape slowly into open and avoid it touching anything else and take out sticks covering sticky parts one by one as u open it 
- Open it fully then put it on patient 

Freezing 
- Put regular needle onto syringe and draw up lido 
- Put on smaller gauge needle and start superficial freezing
- Put on bigger gauge needle and do deep freezing, as you go in keep negative pressure to know when in space 
- When in space, start injecting rest of lido as you pull back

Cath 
- While waiting for freezing, get your tubing ready. Remove covers and turn stop cock towards drain so that it’s locked when inserted. Can also put drainage bag onto bed now as don’t need to be sterile and remove the cover from the bag. Also fold two pieces of Tegaderm to get the ready 
- Once ready, use 2 hands to insert tip of Cath at the preset angle 
- Once tip in, need pressure/ pushing in but also under negative pressure 
- *** if having trouble, use one hand to spread/stretch skin apart, *** try letting go of pressure and pushing again 
- Keep pulling back as you go in and when get flashback, advance a bit more. 
- Draw up diagnostic sample
- Start advancing the sheath while keeping needle in place. *** Initially, push all the sheath in and can pull it back as needed. 
- Once sheath is in, withdraw needle and quickly cover with ur thumb to avoid leak
- Connect tubing 

Draining
- Tent the catheter tip with the Tegaderms to stabilize
- Turn stopcock perpendicular to direction of flow to start draining
- On the proximal end of the tubing, there is a blue clip that you can attach to the side of the bed to maintain a horizontal position of the tube test draining.
- Put the drainage back on the floor and can hang it/clip it to the side of the bed all the way down near the floor

Taking sample
- Cell count and differential go in pink top tube. Need blunt fill needle to be able to easily drain into that pink top tube

<u>Albumin</u>
Give if cirrhotic (since they have bad hemodynamics/ vasoD) with LVP >5L
	can also consider for smaller volume <5L if high risk for AKI/ hypotension
Dosing
	25% albumin = 25g in 100mL = 1 bottle
	(note sometimes 25% albumin can also be smaller bottle 12.5g in 50mL) 
	general rule is 6-8g per L removed
		eg. 5L = 40g = just give 2 bottles (50g = 100cc) to not waste
		5-7L = just give 2 bottles
		3-5L (at risk) = just give 1 bottle
