-Instead of simple nephrostomy tube that goes into kidney and drains to outside bag

-Have a “Hobbs” catheter that goes from the kidney and all the way down ureter then drains in the bladder or in ileal conduit if pt had bladder removed (common for bladder/urothelial Ca) 

-Usually put in instead of a nephrostomy, when first put in, they keep the external drainage bag, usually capped after the procedure to allow trial of draining via the Hobbs, then can remove the external drain
