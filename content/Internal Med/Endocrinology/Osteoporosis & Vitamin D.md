```
# ? Osteoporosis
clinical = frag # ie. low trauma major # (hip, vert, humerus, pelvis) OR FRAX(/CAROC) >20% risk (age 50+)
BMD dx = ≤ -2.5
W/u 2ndary causes: Ca, PO4, ALP, TSH, CBC/ SPEP (if verteb #), Cr, 25-OH Vit D

```

Secondary causes workup
	CBC (anemia – myeloma, malabsorption e.g. celiac dz, chronic inflammatory dz)  
	Ca (↑ in primary hyperpara → ↑ bone resorption; ↓/N in secondary hyperpara from vit D deficiency, CKD, malabsorption)  
	Cr (CKD-MBD, Ø bisphos if CrCl <35)  
	ALP (↑ in high bone tunrover - hyperpara, Paget, osteomalacia; ↓ in hypophosphatasia Ø bisphos)  
	TSH (↓ in hyperthyroidism or excess synthroid → ↑ bone resorption)  
	SPEP (M-spike – myeloma rather than typical OP)  
	25-OH Vit D (↓ = ↓ Ca absorption = secondary hyperpara, osteomalacia; replete before antiresorptives)  
	PO4 (↓ in hyperpara, vit D deficiency, renal PO4 wasting → osteomalacia; ↑ in CKD-MBD sec hyperpara)

Tx
	Tx indications (≥50yo): hip/vert #, ≥2 fragility #, FRAX ≥20%, or age ≥70 + T-score ≤−2.5  
	+/- Tx if FRAX 15–19% or age <70 + T-score ≤−2.5  
	Nonpharm: Ca 1000–1200 mg/d (diet preferred), Vit D 400+ IU/d (total 600–800 IU/d), resistance/balance exercise, falls prevention  
	1st line: bisphos (alendronate 70 mg PO weekly, risedronate 35 mg PO weekly, or zoledronic acid 5 mg IV yearly)  
	2nd line: denosumab 60 mg SC q6mo (if bisphos contraindicated/intolerant; Ø abrupt cessation → rebound verteb #)  
	Severe vert # or multiple vert # + T-score ≤−2.5 → Endo for anabolic Tx (teriparatide/romosozumab)  
	Duration: bisphos 3–6 yrs then reassess +/- drug holiday; repeat BMD ~3 yrs after starting Tx
