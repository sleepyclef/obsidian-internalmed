who should we worry about getting invasive fungal disease
	immunocompromised = steroids (or other meds), chemo (esp during periods of nutropenia), transplant, HIV (advanced), DM (poorly controlled)
	sometimes immunocompetent pts can still have it, for example cryptococcus gad 
	for invasive pulm dz, specific risk factor is structurally abN lung due to more freq exposure to fungi and thus more likely to be colonized by fungi which can then go on to cause invasive pulmonary disease

candidemia = not a lot of guess work, will grow in the usual bcx set that is sent

when to suspect funal PNA
	immunocompromised, structural lung dz
	not responding to typical PNA tx 
	imaging 
		- tree in bud - can also be seen with TB, should order fungal and mycobacterial ix 
		- cavities + fungal ball (the fungal ball in itself would not count as invasive but if thinking there is invasive dz then can help support)
		- nodules esp in immunocompromised pts, esp also nodules in the presence of LAD (lymphadenopathy)
		- calcified nodules or lymph nodes may indicate chronicity
		- halo sign (nodule with area of ground glass around it) or reverse halo sign (hazy in mid with more intense around it) - associated with aspergillosus but not specific for invasive fungal disease, can also be seen with mycobacterial and bacterial infection
	exposures
		- soil/ dead leaves - fungi likes to live in composting material (eg. gardening, esp with constant/ ongoing exposure)  = aspergillus 
		- construction = aspergillus + blasto
		- bird and bat droppings = histo 
		- south west USA - dry env (eg. Arizona) = cocci 
workup 
	- good imaging - HRCT
	- need to get resp sample, ideally bronch
		send for sputum bacterial C&S, sputum AFB smear + myobacterial cx x3, fungal cx, galactomannan, PJP PCR
	- blood cultures - will pick up fungal pathogens
	- blasto + histo urine and blood Ag (serology not helpful in acute setting) - if find the Ag in blood does Ø mean blood stream infection, only growing the organism growing in bcx will tell you that
	- cocci serology (no Ag test available)
	- galactomannan blood (but more sens in resp sample than blood)
	- (beta D glucan is hard to access here in Canada)
tx
	empiric if concerned about this (eg. ICU pt, terrible PNA, possible immunocomp/ nodules or other concerning imaging findings)
		amphotericin (liposomal safer, betetr s/e profile) is great empiric choice - broad spectrum for a lot of fungal infxns
