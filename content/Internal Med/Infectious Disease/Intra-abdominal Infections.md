Duration of abx
STOP-IT trial (NEJM 2015) = 4d of abx after source control 
	pts w complicated intra-abdominal infections who have undergone adequate source control (drainge, OR) 
		complicated = extends beyond the organ of origin into the peritoneal space, resulting in an intra-abdo collection/abscess and/or peritonitis.
		source control starts whenever there is connection between the collection and the environment, ie. drain put in place etc.
	4d abx post vs longer until two days after fever/leukocytosis resolves 
	Ø difference in composite outcome
	>> 1/6 pts had recurrence w/in 28d - did not matter how long they got the abx for
		pts with shorter abx course would declare sooner if recurrence occured
		∴  longer/ extended abx does Ø prevent recurrence, only delays it
	Dr. Morris (ID) - extrapolates this to other infxns as well after source control is achieved (eg. lung abscess, pleural eff)
