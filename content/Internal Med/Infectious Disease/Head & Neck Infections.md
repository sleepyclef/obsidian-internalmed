

<u>Acute tonsillitis</u>
w/u
	-Throat swab C&S, monospot, rapid strep, CRP 
	-CT H&N if significant sx to r/o mass or abscess
	-make sure can swallow and no airway obstruction
	-examine for meningitis if headache/neck pain (sx of both) 
mx
	-Amoxicillin 500mg PO BID x10d
	-Naproxen 500mg BID PRN (M: 20 tabs) 
	-if getting worse after 48 hours, review and assess for abscess or peritonsillar sepsis. Look for asymmetrical unilateral signs, worsening throat pain, difficulty swallowing, ear pain, and lateral neck swelling. If present, consider IV antibiotics & admission.

Infectious mononucleosis
```
# Mono
Usually pharyngitis +/- tonsillitis 
Monospot (heterophile antibodies), EBV serology (VCA IgM)
Liver enzymes, CBC lymphocytosis r/o ↓ Hb/ plt   
CT H&N w contrast if airway compromise 
Abdo US not routine, can be done later prior to return to sports
Tylenol, ibuprofen 400mg PO q6-8h/QID PRN, ketorlac 10mg IV/IM q6h PRN
Dex 10mg PO/IV x1 then 4mg q6h short course (or equivalent) if severe tonsil, airway comp, hemolytic anemia/thrombocytopenia 
No contact sport or heavy lifting 3-4wks (risk splenic rupture) 
Limit transmission by Ø sharing drinks/utensils during 2-4wk infx period or as long as sx 
Admit it airway issue, dehydration, Ø PO, hepatic or heme comp 
Consult ENT if peritonsillar abscess; Gen surg or IR if splenic rupture
```
